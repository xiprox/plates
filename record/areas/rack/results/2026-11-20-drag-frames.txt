The rack's frames while its window moves on every frame, on macOS 26.5.2, 2026-11-20, on a
MacBook Pro with an Apple M1 Pro. Its built-in display runs at up to 120Hz and is the primary
screen at 2x, and a 3440x1440 display beside it runs at up to 100Hz at 1x. Every run below
showed the rack on the built-in display, where a refresh lasts 8.3ms.

The probe was a temporary change to Plates, not kept. `plates showcase` opened the rack on its
made-up tiles, waited 1.5s for the tiles to grow in, then moved the window around a circle of
radius 120pt, half a turn a second, on every frame for 4s, with no mouse. The circle puts the
window at fractions of a point, as a throw's glide does, and as a drag does when the pointer
moves by fractions of a point (unverified; the last section has one reading at rest).
Each frame logged when the rack's render began, and each move logged when the task that calls
setFrame:display: ran and how long the call took. In the 440-frame run before the fix, and in
every release run after it, a copy of gpui-pre-macos 0.3.12, patched in for the probe, also
logged GPUI's own events into the same file: each Metal display
link update, each windowDidMove, each call of setFrameSize on GPUI's view (logged on entry,
before GPUI's check that the size changed), each displayLayer, and each restart of the display
link.

The numbers cover 3.8s of the move. "Late" counts the gaps between frames over 9ms, longer than
one 8.3ms refresh, and over 17ms, longer than two, where at least one refresh got no new frame.
"Draw" is from the start of a frame's render to the move task running after it, which spans
GPUI's layout, its paint, and its hand-off of the frame to Metal.

Where AppKit puts a window: Swift scripts, apart from Plates, opened a borderless 304x400pt
window on the built-in display and moved it with setFrame:display: through runs of 400 rects,
then read back the window's rect after each move. In the first run, each rect was computed as
Plates' to_ns computes one, from a rect in pixels whose right edge is its left edge plus 608px
and whose bottom is its top plus 800px, starting at a left edge of 600px and a top of 300px and
stepping 0.731px right and 0.377px down each move.

  rects as computed        widths 303.99999999999994 to 304.00000000000006pt, heights
                           399.99999999999994 to 400.00000000000006pt; 232 of 400 not exactly
                           304x400pt
  window sizes afterward   304x400: 272   305x400: 67   304x401: 48   305x401: 13
  of those                 the 168 rects that asked exactly 304x400pt all kept it; 128 of the other 232
                           came out a point wider, taller, or both
  snapped to half points   304x400: 400, every origin a whole point, 300 origins moved from
                           what was asked
  snapped to whole points  304x400: 400, every origin a whole point, none moved from what was
                           asked
